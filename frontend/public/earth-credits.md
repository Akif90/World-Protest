# Globe surface

`earth.webp` is a 2048 × 1024 equirectangular, stylized Earth surface (about 57 KB).
Coastlines derive from world-atlas 2.0.2 land-50m, based on Natural Earth
public-domain geographic data. Colors and subtle procedural variation are
illustrative, not satellite imagery or measured elevation.

The surface is shared by both themes and cached with the app shell. No remote
tiles, font downloads, or additional rendering dependencies are required.
